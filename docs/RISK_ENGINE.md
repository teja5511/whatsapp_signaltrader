## Validation Issue Codes

- `ENTRY_COUNT_BELOW_MINIMUM` / `ENTRY_COUNT_ABOVE_MAXIMUM`
- `LOT_SIZE_NON_POSITIVE` / `LOT_SIZE_BELOW_SYMBOL_MINIMUM` / `LOT_SIZE_ABOVE_SYMBOL_MAXIMUM`
- `VOLUME_STEP_MISMATCH`
- `TOTAL_VOLUME_EXCEEDED` (exceeding max exposure cap `2.0000` lots)
- `STOP_LOSS_MISSING` / `STOP_LOSS_DIRECTION_INVALID`
- `SIGNAL_TP_DIRECTION_INVALID`
- `HUNDRED_PIP_POLICY_MISSING`
- `TP_INDEX_POLICY_MISSING`
- `ORDER_INTENT_UNRESOLVED`
- `CAMPAIGN_STATE_NOT_PLANNABLE`
- `TRADING_ENABLED_UNEXPECTEDLY`
